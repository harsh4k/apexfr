1. Overview

Late Apex (“we,” “us,” “our”) is a web design & development studio. This privacy policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

We respect your privacy and comply with applicable data protection laws, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP), the Information Technology Act 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules 2011, the EU’s General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK Data Protection Act 2018, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) & California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

2. What Data We Collect

We collect data in the following ways:

  • Contact Form: Name, email, project description, budget, and any other details you provide.
  • Newsletter Signup: Name and email address.
  • Service Delivery: During project work, we collect information needed to build and deliver your website or web application, including design preferences, content, domain details, and access credentials you share with us.
  • Payment: Payment information is handled by Razorpay (India) and Stripe (international). We do not store full credit card details; payment processors retain payment data under their privacy policies.
  • Site Usage: If we enable privacy-friendly analytics in future (e.g., Plausible, Fathom), we may collect page views, referrer source, and browser type—without cookies or user identification. No tracking pixels, ads, or third-party data brokers.
  • Communication: Email correspondence, support tickets, and meeting notes related to your project.

3. Why We Collect & Process Your Data

We process your personal data on these lawful bases:

  • Contract Performance: To deliver your project, process payments, and provide ongoing support (e.g., bug fixes, care plan services).
  • Consent: For newsletter signups and optional analytics. You can withdraw consent anytime by unsubscribing or contacting us.
  • Legitimate Business Interests: To improve our services, respond to inquiries, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations—balanced against your privacy rights.
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with tax, employment, and data protection laws (e.g., India’s GST & IT rules).

4. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Project Data: Retained for 7 years after project completion for legal & tax compliance (India’s GST & income tax rules).
  • Newsletter Subscribers: Kept until you unsubscribe. Unsubscribe links are included in every email.
  • Payment Records: Retained for 7 years per India’s GST & accounting requirements.
  • Support Communications: Retained for 3 years or until dispute resolution is complete.
  • Analytics Data (if enabled): Retained for 12 months then deleted by the analytics provider.

After retention periods expire, we delete or anonymize your data, except where law requires us to keep it longer.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

We only share your data with trusted third parties, bound by confidentiality:

  • Payment Processors: Razorpay (India: https://razorpay.com/privacy) & Stripe (International: https://stripe.com/privacy). They process payments under their own privacy policies.
  • Hosting Providers: We may host your website on platforms like Vercel, AWS, or similar. Your site data & analytics may be stored on their servers in India or abroad.
  • Email Service: If we use services like Mailchimp, Substack, or similar for newsletters, subscriber emails are shared with them. These providers have their own privacy policies.
  • Analytics (if enabled): Privacy-friendly analytics tools like Plausible or Fathom process anonymized page view data.
  • Legal Requirement: We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or government agency.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties for marketing or profit.

6. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to, processed in, or stored in countries outside India, including the EU, UK, USA, and others, for hosting, analytics, or payment processing.

For EU/UK residents (GDPR): We ensure transfers are adequately protected under Chapter 5 of the GDPR, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with our service providers or relying on adequacy decisions for countries like the EU.

For all users: Transfer is only to service providers strictly necessary for your project delivery.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Deletion (Right to be Forgotten): You can request deletion of your data, except where we must retain it for legal reasons.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a structured, portable format suitable for transfer to another service.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: If you consented to processing (e.g., newsletter), you can withdraw that consent anytime.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can ask us to limit how we use your data while we verify or resolve disputes.
  • Right to Object: You can object to processing based on legitimate business interests.

For California residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to opt-out of any “sale” of personal information (though we do not sell data) and to request deletion, access, and correction.

8. How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of the above rights, contact us at hello@lateapex.studio with:

  • Your name and email address.
  • A clear description of your request (e.g., “access,” “deletion,” “correction”).
  • Any relevant project or order details.

We will respond within 30 days (GDPR & DPDP timelines). If you are an EU/UK resident, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with data, we will delete it promptly.
