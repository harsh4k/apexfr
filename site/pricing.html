Pit questions

Before you ask.

How do payments work?

50% to start, 50% at launch. Full builds are split into milestones, each paid only after you approve it on the preview link.

What if I need more than the plan covers?

You get a written quote for the extra before any of it is built. Nothing is added to an invoice without your yes.

Do I pay for hosting?

Yes, directly to the provider, at cost. Most sites run on free or ₹0–1,500/month tiers; I'll tell you which before we start.

Can you work with my designer?

Yes. Send the Figma file and I'll quote the build only, usually 30–40% less than the full plan.

What happens after the 30-day warranty?

Either the Pit crew care plan, or pay-as-you-go changes at ₹1,000/hr. You're never locked in.
