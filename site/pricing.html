    Questions

    Before you ask.

    What counts as a page?

    Any screen with its own address. Repeated items, like 30 products or blog posts, use one template page plus the CMS, not 30 pages.

    How do payments work?

    50% to start and 50% at launch. Custom builds are split into milestones, each paid after you approve it on a preview link.

    I'm outside India. How do you bill?

    In USD by bank transfer or card. Contracts follow the terms of service, with disputes handled by arbitration.

    What if I need more later?

    You get a written quote for the change before anything is built, at the same rates as above.

    Do I pay for hosting?

    Yes, directly to the provider, at cost. Most small sites run on free or low-cost tiers; you'll know which before we start.

    Can I get a refund?

    See the refund and cancellation policy. In short: the advance covers work already started; milestones are refunded for work not yet begun.
