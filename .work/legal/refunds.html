1. Refund Policy Overview

This policy explains when and how we refund payments for web design & development projects and our optional monthly care plan.

Our refund policy is governed by these terms along with India’s Consumer Protection Act 2019 and applicable laws in your jurisdiction (GDPR for EU/UK, CCPA for California, etc.).

2. Project Refunds

2.1 Advance Payment

The advance payment (typically 50% of the project fee) is non-refundable once design & development work has begun.

Exceptions:

  • We cancel the project: If we cancel without cause, we refund the advance minus any costs we’ve incurred (e.g., third-party tools, hosting setup).
  • We cannot deliver: If we are unable to complete the project due to circumstances beyond your control, a pro-rata refund may be issued for work not completed.
  • Major scope change: If you request a major change that fundamentally alters the project scope, we may renegotiate the fee and issue a partial refund or credit.

2.2 Final Payment

The final payment (typically 50% of the project fee) is non-refundable after the website or app has been launched and transferred to your servers.

Exception: If the delivered work does not meet the agreed-upon specifications outlined in the project proposal and cannot be fixed within the 30-day warranty period, you may request a partial refund. We will assess the shortfall and propose either a refund or remedial work at no additional cost.

2.3 Milestone-Based Payments

For larger projects with multiple milestone payments:

  • Payments for completed milestones are non-refundable.
  • If you cancel before a milestone is reached, you pay for work completed up to that point. Advance payment for the uncompleted milestone is refunded, minus any third-party costs incurred (hosting setup, licenses, etc.).
  • If we fail to deliver a milestone on the agreed date (beyond reasonable delays due to your feedback), we may offer a partial refund or credit toward future work.

2.4 Cancellation by Client

You may request to cancel your project at any time by sending written notice to hello@lateapex.studio.

  • If design has not started: We refund the advance payment minus any third-party costs incurred (e.g., domain registration, initial hosting setup).
  • If design is in progress: The advance is non-refundable. We provide work-in-progress files in a standard format (e.g., Figma design, code repository).
  • If development is underway or complete: No refund is issued. You pay for work completed; we provide all deliverables and transfer code ownership upon final payment.

2.5 Cancellation by Late Apex

We reserve the right to cancel a project if:

  • Payment is overdue by 30 days or more.
  • You violate our terms or engage in unethical behavior (harassment, copyright infringement, etc.).
  • Circumstances beyond our control make delivery impossible (business closure, force majeure, etc.).

In such cases, we will provide you with work-in-progress files and offer a pro-rata refund for unstarted work, minus costs already incurred.

3. Monthly Care Plan Cancellation

Our optional care plan is billed monthly in advance and can be cancelled anytime.

  • Cancellation Notice: Send a cancellation request to hello@lateapex.studio at least 5 business days before your next billing date.
  • Refund of Current Month: No refund is issued for the current billing month, regardless of when you cancel. Your plan remains active until the end of that month.
  • Future Billing Stopped: Once we process your cancellation, we will not charge you in the following month. Your plan ends on the last day of the current billing period.
  • Data & Access: After cancellation, we will continue to maintain your website on the agreed hosting plan. Maintenance and updates beyond the care plan become your responsibility or can be purchased as additional support.

Note: Care plan refunds are not available under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 for digital services, as they are ongoing subscription services. However, if we fail to provide services outlined in the plan, you may dispute the charge with your payment provider (chargeback) or seek resolution through our dispute process.

4. 30-Day Bug-Fix Warranty & Refunds

The 30-day bug-fix warranty (described in the Terms of Service) is not a refund guarantee. Instead, we will fix covered bugs at no additional cost.

  • If we cannot fix a bug: We will document the issue and explain the cause. If the bug is due to our error and prevents the site from functioning as specified, we may offer a partial refund or credit for future work.
  • If you want a refund instead of a fix: You may request a refund for the final payment if the delivered site has major defects that make it unusable. We will assess the defect, attempt to fix it, and only if the site remains non-functional may we issue a refund.
  • After 30 days: Any bugs discovered after the warranty period are considered support requests and billed separately (or included in a care plan).

5. Refunds via Payment Processors

All refunds are processed through the payment processor used for the original transaction.

Razorpay (India):

  • Refund requests are submitted to Razorpay.
  • Razorpay processes refunds within 5–7 business days.
  • Refunds return to the original payment method (bank account, credit/debit card, UPI).
  • Your bank may take an additional 2–3 business days to credit the refund to your account.

Stripe (International):

  • Refund requests are submitted to Stripe.
  • Stripe processes refunds within 5–10 business days.
  • Refunds return to the original payment method (credit/debit card, bank account).
  • Your bank or card issuer may take additional time (3–5 business days) to credit the refund.

Bank Transfers:

  • Refunds are initiated by us within 7 business days of approval.
  • International bank transfers may take 5–10 business days depending on intermediary banks.
  • Currency conversion fees (if any) may be deducted by banks.

We have no control over processing times once the refund is submitted to the payment processor. Please contact your bank or payment provider if a refund does not appear within the stated timeframe.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, follow these steps:

  1. Contact us: Email hello@lateapex.studio with your request, including your project name, order/invoice number, and reason for the refund.
  2. We will review: We will assess your request within 3 business days and respond with a decision (approved, partial, or denied) and an explanation.
  3. If approved: We will initiate the refund through the original payment processor (or an alternative method if the original is no longer available).
  4. Refund processing: The payment processor will handle the refund (typically 5–7 business days). Your bank may take additional time to credit your account.
  5. Refund confirmation: Once the refund is processed, we will send you a confirmation with the refund amount, date, and transaction reference.

We aim to resolve all refund requests fairly and promptly.

7. Non-Refundable Items

The following are non-refundable:

  • Third-party costs already paid (hosting, domains, SSL certificates, plugin licenses). These are paid directly to the provider; we act as your agent.
  • Work-in-progress files or drafts if you cancel after design has begun.
  • Consultation or discovery phase fees (if charged separately).
  • Rush fees, expedited delivery fees, or any premium service add-ons.
  • Care plan fees for completed months (see Section 3).

8. Consumer Protection & Your Rights

For Indian Consumers: You have rights under the Consumer Protection Act 2019, including the right to file a complaint with the National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC) if you believe we have provided defective or unsafe services.

For EU/UK Consumers: Under GDPR and consumer protection laws, you may have the right to withdraw from digital services within 14 days of purchase, with limited exceptions. However, custom web development is generally exempt from cooling-off periods once work has begun.

For California Consumers: Under CCPA, you have the right to know, access, delete, and opt out regarding personal data. This policy concerns financial transactions; see our Privacy Policy for data rights.

If you have a dispute with our refund policy, you may:

  • File a formal complaint with your national consumer protection authority.
  • Initiate a chargeback with your payment provider (bank or credit card company).
  • Pursue arbitration or legal action under Section 14 of our Terms of Service.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this refund policy at any time. Changes apply to new projects immediately; refund requests for ongoing projects will be evaluated under the policy in place when the project agreement was signed.

We will notify you of material changes via email or a notice on our website.
